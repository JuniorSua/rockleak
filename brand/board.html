The leak is oil, not blood.

Three refinements of concept 4. The dome stays, the red drop goes. Information now leaks as glossy black oil with a restrained oil-slick sheen, the brand's only accent. The new wordmark is an architectural geometric grotesque with open tracking; its A is a pediment, with a drop where the crossbar would be.
